## The scope

**I managed design and research across a hybrid cloud portfolio from 2019 to 2023.** The products let teams that run z/OS use cloud-style tooling, such as APIs, containers, and DevOps pipelines, alongside their existing workloads. The role combined team leadership, staffing advocacy, and design and research support for multiple products at once.

## My contribution

**I advocated for headcount and grew the core team from 2 to 8 people, with a peak of 18.** The argument was the portfolio itself: more products in flight than two people could research and design for. Growth was staged with the roadmap, so each product had design and research capacity before its release work started.

**I managed through product leads as the team grew.** Each product had a lead who owned its design and research. My job became staffing, priorities across products, and the conversations with product management and engineering about what the team could take on.

## Team delivery

**The team shipped four hybrid cloud products to general availability.** These included the **IBM Z and Cloud Modernization Stack**. Product delivery was a team outcome. My responsibilities covered design and research management and the organizational work described above.

## Evidence and limits

**Team growth and general availability are the before-and-after evidence.** Product telemetry is not included in this account. The evidence describes organizational capacity and delivery milestones, without attributing changes in adoption or revenue to the work.
