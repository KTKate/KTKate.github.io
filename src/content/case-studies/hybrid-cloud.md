## The scope

**I managed design and research across a hybrid cloud portfolio from 2019 to 2023.** The role combined team leadership, staffing advocacy, and support for multiple enterprise products.

## My contribution

**I advocated for headcount and grew the core team from 2 to 8 people, with a peak of 18.** This established more design and research capacity for the portfolio.

## Team delivery

**The team shipped four hybrid cloud products to general availability.** These included the **Z and Cloud Modernization Stack**. Product delivery was a team outcome; my responsibilities covered design and research management and the organizational work described above.

## Evidence and limits

**Team growth and general availability are the available before-and-after evidence.** Product telemetry is not included in this account. The evidence therefore describes organizational capacity and delivery milestones, without attributing changes in adoption or revenue to the work.
