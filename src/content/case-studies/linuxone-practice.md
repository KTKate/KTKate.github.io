## The organizational need

**LinuxONE needed a dedicated design and research practice.** I founded it and grew it from **zero to six people**, creating capacity for research and design across LinuxONE and Linux on Z.

## My decisions

**I established recurring client participation.** I founded the LinuxONE client council with about **30 clients**, recurring virtual sessions, and one in-person event each year. Its purpose is design input and future direction, not sales.

**I connected the practice to product definition.** I lead design and research on Nexus and perform product leadership work without the product manager title, with influence across product management, strategy, and engineering.

## Team activity and evidence

**The practice supports ongoing research and design; the council provides recurring client input.** Team members contribute to that work. I established the practice and council and lead the product and platform direction described here.

**The evidence is organizational and qualitative.** Practice size and recurring client participation show what was established. This describes an organizational capability rather than a product with its own telemetry.
